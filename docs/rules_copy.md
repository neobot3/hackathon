# Scheduling Rules

Rules for scheduling Tech Reps, from two sources:

- **CBA:** the WestJet / AMFA collective agreement, 2024-2029 ([CBA.txt](CBA.txt)). Clause numbers are in brackets.
- **Meeting:** the scheduler meeting with Dave and Jason on October 16, 2026 ([Meeting.vtt](Meeting.vtt)).

Tech Reps (Technical Representative and Technical Representative Lead) are a Group III classification [18-4.01, 18-4.02]. CBA rules below are limited to those that apply to Group III or to scheduling in general.

---

## CBA Rules

### Seniority

| Rule | Clause |
| --- | --- |
| Union Seniority is cumulative service in covered classifications. Training counts as time worked. | 13-1.01 |
| On equal seniority dates, the lower employee number is more senior. | 13-1.03 |
| The Master Seniority List is published every February 1 and August 1. | 13-2.01 |
| Seniority is forfeited on leaving the company, layoff over 3 years, or more than 24 months in a non-union role, among others. A rehired employee keeps the employee number but not the seniority. | 13-4.01 |

## Meeting Rules

### Schedule Building

- Build each rep's schedule at least 30 days ahead. In mid-October, November is fully plotted.
- The Heavy Maintenance Schedule drives where reps go. Dave often hears about changes before the schedule is updated.
- Reps work 7-on/7-off rotations at the MROs.
- The goal is a full-year schedule built from awarded vacation, qualifications, home base, sick time and training.

### Days Off and Rest

- Each set of days off needs at least 48 hours off. The free days need not be consecutive. Example: training on the 2nd to 4th, off the 5th, work the 6th to 9th, off the 10th, overtime on the 11th.
- After a run of work or overtime, a rep rests 2 days before more overtime.

### Training

- Training is mostly scheduled on days off, as overtime.
- On shift, training must fall on the first or last day of the rotation. In the middle, the rep would have to leave the MRO and fly back. Dave rejects mid-rotation dates.
- Training runs in Calgary, or in Toronto for eastern-based reps.
- Recurrent training (HF, CRM, Hot Topics) is 3 consecutive days, every 2 years. It is needed to hold a WestJet ACA.
- First Aid and Fire Extinguisher training are every 3 years. Nothing is yearly.
- Endorsement courses take 4 to 5 weeks in Calgary. On completion, the aircraft type is added to the rep's endorsements.
- Endorsements are NG, MAX, BCF, Q400 and 787.
- The training department submits dates. The scheduler accepts or rejects them. A rejection goes back with a note.

### Home Base and Coverage

- The Kelowna team covers Kelowna.
- The Toronto team covers the east: JD Aero, AAR in Windsor, and FlightStar in Florida.
- The Calgary team covers the rest, such as Tucson and San Antonio. Calgary has no MRO, so its reps always travel.
- Calgary also backs up the east and covers Kelowna vacation.
- Keep reps as close to home as possible.
- Keep a rep at the same MRO for 2 to 3 rotations instead of moving every week.

### Overtime

- Overtime goes by seniority, not employee number. A rehired rep keeps the old employee number but loses seniority.
- Offer overtime to all reps on days off at the base that covers the work. Award it to the most senior rep who says yes.
- The scheduler cannot hand-pick a rep for overtime based on experience with an aircraft type.
- Overtime can be paid out or banked. Banked time can be used as vacation.

### Vacation and Stat Holidays

- Coordinators post each rep's vacation allotment, in seniority order.
- Bidding has 3 rounds. Rounds 1 and 2 accept only full 7-day blocks.
- At most 3 reps on vacation at once. Clashes go to the most senior.
- Round 3 usually has a few days left over. Reps fill the rest of the rotation with banked time.
- Next year's vacation must be entered by the end of November. If a rep does not bid, the manager picks the vacation.
- After January 1, a rep may move vacation only to a rotation with fewer than 3 people off.
- All vacation must be used by December 31 or it is lost. WestJet has sometimes allowed it in the first quarter.
- Reps get their 11 stat holidays up front and bid them with vacation. They do not get the stat day off automatically.
- A rep may take a stat day in lieu, for example to avoid spending it away from home.
- Taking 7 vacation days gives 21 days off, because of the 7 days off on each side.

### Sick Leave

- 12 paid sick days a year, prorated by month: 12 in January, 11 in February, 10 in March and so on.
- Once paid sick days run out, sick time is unpaid.
- Sick before travel makes the first rotation day a sick day. Sick on site is a sick day at the MRO.
- A rep back from sick leave does not make up the missed days.

### Travel

- A rep travels on day 1 of the rotation, works days 2 to 6, and travels home on day 7.
- A rep may take a travel day on overtime before the rotation.
- Travel days pay a full 12-hour shift, because travel over 4 hours counts as a full shift. Dave was not sure of the CBA clause.
- Kelowna is a 45-minute flight. A rep flies in that morning and works the same day.

### Maintenance Volatility

- Avoid days with no rep at an active MRO between one rep leaving and the next arriving, especially around deliveries.
- Unplanned events: AOG, lease returns, bird strikes, hard landings, hail damage, MRO starts and finishes, and last-minute sales or delivery requests.
- Warn before scheduling a rep at an MRO after its last aircraft of that type returns to service.
- Reps want to know where they are going as far ahead as possible.
